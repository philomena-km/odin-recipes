Lasagna

Simple Ingredients the recipe for lasagna is one that is easy and simple to use.The ingredients are readily available
and they are affordable to everyone. the step by step procedure makes it easy to make it.

Ingredients

    wheat flour sugar oil Yeast

Steps

    sieve the flour into a bowl Add sugar aand salt into the flour Mix all the dry ingredients mix the yeast with water and let it sit Knead the dough
